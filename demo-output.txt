== Reality: the Ptolemy demo.
   Tables: the position of Mars on each day from 2024-Jan-01 to 2025-Dec-30 (730 days),
   from NASA/JPL Horizons, asked twice: once with the observer at Earth, once at the Sun.
   earth = mars-seen-from-earth.tsv  (sha256 ca65570db219...)
   sun   = mars-seen-from-sun.tsv  (sha256 ac15aaffcb4b...)
   The 'motion' column says whether Mars's ecliptic longitude went up (prograde) or down
   (retrograde, 'backward' against the stars) by the next day. convert.py derives it; see SOURCES.md.
   Lines starting with $ are commands; outcome= lines are the program's whole output.
   '(exit N)', 'reads as:' and 'means:' lines are printed by this demo script, not by the program.
   A 'means:' line prints only if the program's line is exactly the one expected.

$ head -3 mars-seen-from-earth.tsv
date	seen_from	ecliptic_longitude_deg	motion
2024-Jan-01	Earth	267.3083422	prograde
2024-Jan-02	Earth	268.0501386	prograde
$ head -3 mars-seen-from-sun.tsv
date	seen_from	ecliptic_longitude_deg	motion
2024-Jan-01	Sun	258.5668659	prograde
2024-Jan-02	Sun	259.1197004	prograde

== 1. Ptolemy's question, in English.
$ cat center.txt
Is the Earth at the center?
$ python3 reality.py propose ledger center.txt mars-seen-from-earth.tsv
outcome=REFUSED rule=F1 condition=not-reality-1 line=1
  (exit 3)
  means: refused: the sentence names no bytes and no measure. Center of what, seen from where, measured how is left unsaid, so it is not a question here

  means: refused: these bytes have no column 'center'. What they have is seen_from: the observer is a column in the data, set when the data was asked for

== 6. Foundations: what each table is said to rest on. Each is signed (namespace reality-foundation-1).
   A foundation is a signed name, plus the foundations it rests on. A tag is a signed claim that a table
   rests on a foundation. Both are commitments by a holder of a listed key: the program cannot tell who
   holds the key, and it cannot tell whether a tag is true of the table.
   The Jupiter table (same source, same days, observer at Earth) is tagged with 'epicycles', which rests on
   'earth-frame'. The demo author chose these names and links.

== 7. Knock down the Earth frame, with the Earth-vs-Sun comparison as live witness.
   undermine takes the comparison and both tables, checks they are the named bytes and the comparison is
   adopted, counts again on the spot, and goes ahead only if the result is DOES-NOT-HOLD. The signer
   must name which side falls. The program does not decide that: the same witness would let a key
   holder name the Sun side instead. It records which side the signer named.
$ cat u-earth.txt
reality 1
undermine
foundation sha256 fba9911a2e3158af2bc62c5328648fb9f41d59b26234768409cbb7213a6a6f60
witness sha256 e5f85caacf988c9cf813eabccb1ecb524800a103ddde7ab78790b71e5f44bc53
$ ssh-keygen -Y sign -f testkey -n reality-undermine-1 u-earth.txt
$ python3 reality.py undermine ledger u-earth.txt u-earth.txt.sig REALITY-TEST-KEY-NOT-PATSAK q-agree.txt mars-seen-from-earth.tsv mars-seen-from-sun.tsv
outcome=UNDERMINED foundation=fba9911a2e3158af2bc62c5328648fb9f41d59b26234768409cbb7213a6a6f60 side=1 measure=e5f85caacf988c9cf813eabccb1ecb524800a103ddde7ab78790b71e5f44bc53 matching-1=79 rows-1=730 matching-2=0 rows-2=730 undermine-line=19
  (exit 11)
  means: undermined: recounted now, 79/730 vs 0/730; the signer knocked down side 1, the Earth frame
